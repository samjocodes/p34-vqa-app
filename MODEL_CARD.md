**Author:** Saumya Joshi (ADT23SOCB0997, Roll 34, AIA-05 Batch B) · **Version:** 1.0 · **Type:** application composed of pre-trained models (no new training)

## Intended use
Educational demonstration of multimodal question answering; exploring failure modes on low-light and cluttered images; showing how an LLM can make short answers readable (e.g. for screen readers).

## Out-of-scope / not for
Medical, legal, safety-critical or navigation decisions; identifying people; inferring gender, age, race, religion, health or other sensitive attributes; surveillance.

## Data
No training data was used or collected. The 20-pair benchmark and the 10 bias silhouettes are generated by `synth.py` (simple shapes, exact ground truth). They are easier than real photographs and are **not** representative of real-world performance. User uploads are processed in memory and not stored by the app.

## Evaluation
Run from the app's *Evaluation and Report* tab (or `python vqa_eval.py`). Metrics: correctness % on 20 image–question pairs (clean / low-light / cluttered), low-light before/after brightening, zero-shot vs few-shot faithfulness, bias test on gender-neutral silhouettes, guardrail block/allow rates. Numbers are generated into `results/results_summary.md` and the PDF report so they are never hand-typed or stale.

## Known limitations and risks
- BLIP-VQA-base is a small model: counting, small objects, dark and cluttered images are error-prone.
- Generative models can produce fluent but wrong answers (hallucination); the app shows a confidence value and flags low confidence.
- Without a guardrail the model gives gendered answers for gender-neutral silhouettes (see measured bias test), reflecting dataset bias; therefore sensitive questions are refused.
- The LLM sentence is checked to contain the original answer; if not, a rule-based sentence replaces it.
- Free CPU hosting: first request is slow (model loading).

## Safety guardrails
Sensitive-attribute / identification questions refused · prompt-injection phrases refused · input size/type/length limits · low-confidence warning · faithfulness fallback · API key kept in platform secrets.

## Ethical, legal and IP notes
Benchmark images are original (generated by the author). Third-party models are used under their licences above. Hosted LLM use (optional) sends only the question and terse answer text, never the image.
